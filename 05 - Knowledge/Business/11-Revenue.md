# 11 — Revenue

> Section 11 of the AI Engineering OS

## Purpose

Tracks actual revenue across all streams, monitors unit economics, forecasts growth.

## Owner Team

Executive

## Key Responsibilities

- Revenue tracking per stream
- Cost monitoring
- ROI analysis
- Financial forecasting
- Budget compliance

## Revenue Streams Tracking

| Stream | Status | Est. Monthly | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------------|-------|
| YouTube AdSense | Active | Varies | Based on CPM and watch time |
| Affiliate Programs | Active | Varies | AI tools, courses, hardware |
| Sponsorships | Prospective | Varies | Tech brands, SaaS companies |
| Online Courses | Planned | Vises | AI engineering curriculum |
| Tool Sales | Planned | Varies | Templates, prompts, scripts |
| Consulting | Prospective | Varies | AI engineering advisory |

### Revenue Stream Details

**YouTube AdSense**
- Track CPM by video topic (AI topics command premium CPMs)
- Monitor RPM (revenue per 1000 views)
- Optimize for mid-roll ad placement in videos >8 min

**Affiliate Programs**
- Maintain active links for all recommended tools
- Track conversion rates per program
- Negotiate custom commission rates as audience grows

**Sponsorships**
- Rate card based on average views and engagement
- Deliverables: dedicated video, integration, or mention
- Require product relevance to AI engineering audience

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Cost Per Video | Total costs / Videos published | Decreasing trend |
| Cost Per Subscriber | Total costs / New subscribers | <$2/subscriber |
| Revenue Per Video | Total revenue / Videos published | Increasing trend |
| LTV:CAC | Customer lifetime value / Acquisition cost | >3:1 |
| RPM | Revenue / (Views / 1000) | >$10 RPM |
| Break-even Videos | Fixed costs / Revenue per video | Track monthly |

### Forecasting Method

1. Baseline from current month's actuals
2. Apply growth rate per stream (conservative, moderate, optimistic)
3. Factor in known upcoming changes (new content series, partnerships)
4. Include seasonal adjustments (Q4 ad spend surge, summer dips)

## Inputs

- Business (01)
- Analytics data (09)
- Cost map (integrations)

## Outputs

- Monthly revenue report
- Budget alerts
- ROI analysis

## Operating Principles

- Track every dollar in and out
- Conservative forecasting, optimistic execution
- Reinvest early revenue into growth
- Diversify revenue streams early
- Know your unit economics cold

## Acceptance Criteria

- Revenue tracked daily, reported monthly
- Costs categorized and monitored against budget
- Forecasts updated monthly with actuals comparison
- Budget alerts configured for overspend thresholds
- Unit economics calculated per revenue stream
