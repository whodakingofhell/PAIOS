# 12 — Roadmap

> Section 12 of the AI Engineering OS

## Purpose

Master roadmap with milestones, timelines, dependencies, and progress tracking.

## Owner Team

Executive + Architecture

## Key Responsibilities

- Milestone planning
- Dependency tracking
- Progress reporting
- Priority management
- Sprint planning

## Roadmap Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation

**Objective:** Establish core infrastructure and initial content pipeline

- Set up YouTube channel and branding
- Establish Discord community
- Create first 10 videos
- Deploy basic automation workflows
- Set up analytics tracking
- Build initial content calendar

### Phase 2: Growth

**Objective:** Scale content production and audience

- Publish 50+ videos
- Grow to 1,000 subscribers
- Launch newsletter
- First affiliate revenue
- Expand to secondary platforms (Dev.to, LinkedIn)
- Implement SEO strategy
- First sponsorship outreach

### Phase 3: Scale

**Objective:** Systematize operations and increase revenue

- Publish 200+ videos
- Grow to 10,000 subscribers
- Launch online course
- Achieve $1,000+/month revenue
- Hire first team member or contractor
- Automate content distribution
- Establish brand partnerships

### Phase 4: Brand

**Objective:** Become recognized authority in AI engineering space

- Publish 500+ videos
- Grow to 50,000+ subscribers
- Multiple revenue streams active ($10,000+/month)
- Launch premium tooling or products
- Speaking engagements
- Book or comprehensive course launch
- Community-driven content

```
Video Pipeline
├── Recording ──→ Editing ──→ Publishing ──→ Distribution
│                                  │
│                                  ▼
│                           SEO Optimization
│                                  │
│                                  ▼

Audience Growth
├── Content Quality ──→ Watch Time ──→ Algorithm Push
├── SEO ──→ Discovery ──→ New Viewers
└── Community ──→ Retention ──→ Word of Mouth

Revenue
├── Audience Size ──→ AdSense Threshold
├── Content Authority ──→ Sponsorship Deals
```

### Critical Path Items

1. Content pipeline must be operational before growth phase
2. Analytics must be in place before optimization
3. Minimum audience required before sponsorship outreach
4. Revenue thresholds must be met before hiring

## Operating Principles

- Review and update roadmap weekly
- Ruthlessly prioritize — say no to good ideas
- Dependencies identified before work begins
- Progress is measured by outcomes, not activity
- Adjust timelines based on actual velocity, not optimism
- Keep backlog honest — remove items that won't get done

## Acceptance Criteria

- Roadmap reviewed and updated weekly
- All milestones have target dates and assigned owners
- Dependencies documented before sprint commitment
- Progress metrics tracked and visible
- Backlog prioritized and pruned monthly
- Sprint retrospectives conducted after each sprint
