# UX Research

## When to Do UX Research
- **Discovery** (Phase 0): Problem exploration, user needs, competitive analysis
- **Concept** (Phase 1): Validate product direction, test assumptions
- **Design** (Phase 2): Usability testing of prototypes
- **Pre-launch** (Phase 4): UAT, final usability validation
- **Post-launch** (Phase 6): Satisfaction surveys, analytics review, identify friction

## Research Methods

### Generative (discover what to build)
- **User interviews** — 1:1 structured/semi-structured conversations. 5-8 users uncovers ~80% of issues
- **Contextual inquiry** — observe users in their environment
- **Diary studies** — users log experiences over time
- **Competitive analysis** — evaluate competitor strengths/weaknesses

### Evaluative (test what you built)
- **Usability testing** — moderated/unmoderated. Tasks + observation. Measure: task success, time on task, error rate, satisfaction (SUS score)
- **A/B testing** — compare two versions on live traffic
- **Card sorting** — information architecture validation
- **Tree testing** — navigation findability test
- **Heuristic evaluation** — expert review against 10 usability heuristics (Nielsen)

### Continuous
- **Analytics** — funnel analysis, drop-off points, heatmaps (Hotjar, Clarity)
- **Surveys** — NPS, CSAT, CES at key touchpoints
- **Feedback widgets** — in-app feedback, bug reports
- **Session recordings** — watch real user sessions, identify confusion

## Process
1. Define research question (what do we need to learn?)
2. Choose method (qualitative or quantitative)
3. Recruit participants (representative of target users)
4. Conduct sessions (record with consent)
5. Analyze (affinity mapping, theme identification)
6. Report (findings, recommendations, severity)
7. Hand off to design (actionable changes)

## Artifacts
- Personas: 3-5 archetypes with goals, pain points, behaviors
- Journey maps: current-state and future-state
- Empathy maps: says, thinks, does, feels
- User stories: "As a [persona], I want to [goal] so that [reason]"
- Research report: executive summary + methodology + findings + recommendations

## Usability Testing Setup (minimum viable)
- 5 participants per round
- 5-8 tasks covering critical flows
- Measure: success rate, time, errors, satisfaction
- Remote unmoderated: Maze, UserTesting
- Remote moderated: Zoom + screen share
